When Cabure Bonugli put out the call for the Animals show, I knew that I wanted to draw a Mexican gray wolf. Of course the wolf is a frequent fairy tale character, but I had in mind the wolf that my sister Betsy's friend adopted after my father died in 2010, [Zeke (M1089)](https://www.californiawolfcenter.org/news-feed/in-memoriam-zeke). It was almost like my sister believed that the wolf was an incarnation of our father. After Zeke was relocated to the [Calfiornia Wolf Center](https://www.californiawolfcenter.org/) in Julian, California, Betsy & I had the opportunity to visit him there in November of 2013.
